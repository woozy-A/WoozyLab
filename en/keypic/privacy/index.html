Privacy Policy

KeyPic does not collect personal data.

  • KeyPic processes photos locally in folders selected by the user on their Mac.
  • Photos are not uploaded to a server.
  • No login account is required.
  • KeyPic does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party data collection SDKs.
